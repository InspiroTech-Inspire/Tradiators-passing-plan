Treat the 60 days as 42 trading days, and plan to finish in about 30.
The extra 12 days are your buffer for bad weeks, news, or a stretch where your setups don’t show up. That means aiming for about $40 a day on average, or $200 to $250 a week (roughly 2 to 2.5%). If you’re behind pace at day 30, do not raise risk to catch up. That is how most people blow this stage. Keep the same risk and use the remaining time.

Your own limits should sit well inside the firm’s.

Risk per trade: 0.5% ($50) as your default, 0.75% at most for A+ setups. At 0.5% you need about 24R of net profit for 12%. At 1:2 reward and a 45% win rate that’s roughly 65 to 70 trades, or about 1.5 trades a day over a 30-day pace. That’s very doable and it means a single trade never matters.
Daily stop: -1.5% ($150) in Phase 1. Once hit, close everything and walk away. The firm’s 4% is a line you should never be near.
Drawdown ladder: at -2% from your high, drop to 0.25% risk. At -4%, stop trading for the day and review the journal before the next trade. This keeps you away from the $600 limit, which 12 straight losses at 0.5% would barely reach.
Floating losses: many firms count open trades toward the daily limit, so judge your stop by equity, not closed P/L.

Phase 1 pacing

Weeks 1 to 2: base risk only, no pushing. Get to +2% or so and confirm the platform’s numbers match your own tracking.
Weeks 3 to 5: if you’re ahead, you can step up to 0.75% on your best setups. Cushion above the starting balance helps most if the drawdown is static, so check that.
Once you hit +12%, stop trading and request Phase 2 immediately. Don’t trade “just one more” with a passed phase.

Habits that decide most of these

Set a hard Friday exit time (an hour before close) so you never get caught holding over the weekend.
Don’t trade the first minutes after high-impact news unless your strategy specifically does.
Log every trade with the setup and risk, and check the drawdown number each night before you plan the next day.
Once you’re within about 1.5% of the target, take only A+ setups at base risk. Many people lose phases right at the finish line by getting greedy or careless.
